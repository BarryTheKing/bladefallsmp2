PvP Techniques

Movement & spacing

  • Vary your left-right strafes in short bursts so your path is harder to predict.
  • Use sprint to control distance, then pause or change direction instead of charging blindly.
  • Keep moving while tracking your opponent; avoid backing straight away for too long.
  • Jump selectively to dodge or clear terrain—constant jumps can make your movement easier to read.
